Ovolar Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ovolar handles information when you use the Ovolar mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Ovolar does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play.

Game progress, best scores, preferences, and similar gameplay data may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your progress and settings.

2. Data sent off your device

In the current version of Ovolar, we do not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data from the app with third parties for advertising or profiling.

3. Advertising

The current version of Ovolar does not contain advertising.

4. Analytics

The current version of Ovolar does not use active third-party analytics services to collect personal information from users.

5. Permissions

Ovolar does not require access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, photos, or other sensitive device data for normal gameplay.

6. Children

Ovolar is a general-audience casual game collection and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because gameplay progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, you can remove this local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Ovolar.

8. Security

We design Ovolar to minimize the amount of user data handled by the app. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the current app architecture avoids collecting personal data that is not needed for gameplay.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Ovolar's features, analytics, advertising, online services, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
